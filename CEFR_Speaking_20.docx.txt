1) B2 — How do you think social media affects relationships?
Question: How do you think social media affects relationships?
Model answer:
I believe social media can strengthen relationships when people use it to stay in touch, but it can also create distance if users become overly dependent on online communication. In my experience, balance is essential.
Pronunciation:
“ai bi-liv so-sial mi-dee-uh ken streng-then ri-lay-shun-ships ven pee-pul yus it tu stey in touch, bet it ken ol-so kri-eyt di-stans if yus-ers bi-kum ow-ver di-pen-dent on on-lain kom-yu-ni-kay-shun. in mai iks-peer-ee-ens, bal-ans is es-en-shal.”
Uzbek meaning:
Menimcha, ijtimoiy media munosabatlarni mustahkamlashi mumkin, lekin agar odamlar internet orqali muloqotga haddan tashqari bog‘lib qolishsa, ular orasida masofa paydo bo‘lishi mumkin. Mening tajribamda muvozanat muhim.

2) B2 — Do you think young people spend too much time online?
Question: Do you think young people spend too much time online?
Model answer:
Yes, I do. Many teenagers are constantly connected to their phones, and this can reduce face-to-face interaction. However, the internet can also be educational and helpful if it is used wisely.
Pronunciation:
“yes, ai du. meni tee-nay-jers ar kon-stant-lee kon-nekt-ed tu der founz, and dis ken ri-dyus feys-tu-feys in-ter-ak-shun. ha-ev-er, di in-ter-net ken ol-so bi ed-yu-kay-shun-al and help-ful if it is yusd wisely.”
Uzbek meaning:
Ha, shunday deb o‘ylayman. Ko‘plab o‘smirlar telefonlari bilan doimiy bog‘liq bo‘lib qoladilar va bu yuzma-yuz muloqotni kamaytiradi. Ammo internet to‘g‘ri ishlatilsa, ta’lim va foyda berishi mumkin.

3) B2 — What are the advantages and disadvantages of automation?
Question: What are the advantages and disadvantages of automation?
Model answer:
The main advantage is efficiency. Machines can perform repetitive tasks faster and with fewer mistakes. On the other hand, automation may reduce the need for certain jobs, which could lead to unemployment in some sectors.
Pronunciation:
“di meyn ad-van-tij iz ef-ish-en-see. ma-shinz ken per-form ri-pet-i-tiv tasks faster and wid fewer mis-tayks. on di uder hand, aw-to-may-shun may ri-dyus di need for ser-tin jobs, which kud liyd tu un-em-ploy-ment in sam sek-tors.”
Uzbek meaning:
Asosiy afzallik samaradorlikdir. Mashinalar takroriy vazifalarni tezroq va kamroq xatolik bilan bajarishi mumkin. Boshqa tomondan, avtomatlashtirish ba’zi kasblarga ehtiyojni kamaytirishi mumkin, bu esa ayrim sohalarda ishsizlikka olib kelishi mumkin.

4) B2 — Is it better to study in a group or alone?
Question: Is it better to study in a group or alone?
Model answer:
I think studying in a group is better for discussion and motivation because you can exchange ideas and learn from others. However, studying alone is sometimes more effective when you need deep concentration.
Pronunciation:
“ai thingk stud-ee-ing in a grup is bet-er for dis-kush-un and mo-ti-vay-shun be-kaz yoo ken iks-chaynj idee-az and lern from ad-ers. ha-ev-er, stud-ee-ing a-loun is sam-times mor ef-ek-tiv when yoo need deep kon-sen-tray-shun.”
Uzbek meaning:
Menimcha, guruhda o‘qish muhokama va motivatsiya uchun yaxshiroq, chunki fikr almashish va boshqalardan o‘rganish mumkin. Ammo o‘zingizga kerak bo‘lsa, yolg‘iz o‘qish chuqur konsentratsiya uchun yanada samarali bo‘lishi mumkin.

5) B2 — What do you think is the biggest problem in cities today?
Question: What do you think is the biggest problem in cities today?
Model answer:
Traffic congestion is one of the biggest problems in modern cities because it wastes time, increases pollution, and reduces the quality of life. A better public transport system could help solve it.
Pronunciation:
“traf-ik kon-jes-shun is wun of di big-est prob-lems in mod-ern sit-eez be-kaz it waysts taym, in-krees-es po-lu-shun, and ri-dyus di kwal-i-tee of laif. a bet-er pub-lik trans-port sis-tem kud help solv it.”
Uzbek meaning:
Tirbandlik zamonaviy shaharlardagi eng katta muammolardan biridir, chunki vaqtni yo‘qotadi, ifloslanishni oshiradi va hayot sifatini pasaytiradi. Yaxshiroq jamoat transporti tizimi buni hal qilishga yordam beradi.

6) B2 — Do you agree that money cannot buy happiness?
Question: Do you agree that money cannot buy happiness?
Model answer:
I partly agree. Money can provide comfort and security, but happiness depends more on relationships, health, and personal fulfilment. Without those, wealth may feel empty.
Pronunciation:
“ai par-tlee a-gree. mon-ee ken pro-vide kom-fort and se-kur-i-tee, bet hap-ee-ness di-penz mor on ri-lay-shun-ships, helth, and per-son-al ful-fill-ment. wid-out doz, welth may feel em-tee.”
Uzbek meaning:
Men qisman roziyman. Pul qulaylik va xavfsizlik berishi mumkin, lekin baxt ko‘proq munosabatlar, sog‘liq va shaxsiy qoniqishni belgilaydi. Bular bo‘lmasa, boylik bo‘sh his qilinishi mumkin.

7) B2 — Should governments do more to protect the environment?
Question: Should governments do more to protect the environment?
Model answer:
Absolutely. Governments have the power to introduce stricter laws, invest in renewable energy, and encourage sustainable lifestyles. Without strong leadership, environmental damage will continue to worsen.
Pronunciation:
“ab-so-lut-lee. gov-ern-ments hav di pow-er tu in-tro-dyus stric-ter laws, in-vest in ri-new-uh-bul en-er-jee, and en-kur-ij sus-tay-nuh-bul lif-stayls. wid-out strong lee-der-ship, en-vi-ron-men-tal da-mij wil kon-tin-yu tu wor-sen.”
Uzbek meaning:
Albatta. Hukumatlar qattiqroq qonunlar joriy etish, qayta tiklanuvchi energiyaga sarmoya kiritish va barqaror turmush tarzini rag‘batlantirishga qodir. Kuchli yetakchilik bo‘lmasa, atrof-muhitga zarar yetkazish davom etaveradi.

8) C1 — How has technology changed the way we learn?
Question: How has technology changed the way we learn?
Model answer:
Technology has transformed learning by making information more accessible, more interactive, and far more personalised. We can now learn at our own pace, access global resources, and collaborate across borders. However, digital distraction remains a serious challenge.
Pronunciation:
“tech-nol-o-jee haz trans-formd lern-ing by may-king in-for-may-shun mor ak-ses-uh-bul, mor in-ter-ak-tiv, and far mor per-son-al-ized. wi ken now lern at our oun peys, ak-ses glo-bul ri-sor-siz, and ko-lab-o-reyt a-kros bor-ders. ha-ev-er, dig-i-tal dis-trak-shun ri-maynz a se-ri-ous chal-enj.”
Uzbek meaning:
Texnologiya ta’limni o‘zgartirib, ma’lumotni yanada qulay, interaktiv va shaxsiylashtirilgan qildi. Endi har bir kishi o‘z tezligida o‘rganishi, global manbalarga kirishi va chet el bilan hamkorlik qilishi mumkin. Shu bilan birga, raqamli chalg‘ituvchi narsalar jiddiy muammo bo‘lib qolmoqda.

9) C1 — Is it possible to balance work and personal life in modern society?
Question: Is it possible to balance work and personal life in modern society?
Model answer:
It is possible, but it requires deliberate choices and strong boundaries. Many people are expected to be constantly available, which can blur the line between professional and personal responsibilities. A healthy balance depends on time management, self-discipline, and realistic expectations.
Pronunciation:
“it is pos-uh-bul, bet it ri-kwires de-li-ber-it choi-siz and strong bound-er-iz. meni pee-pul ar ik-spek-ted tu bi kon-stant-lee avay-luh-bul, which ken blur di lain be-tween pro-fesh-un-al and per-son-al ri-spon-suh-bil-i-tiz. a helthy bal-ans di-penz on taym man-ij-ment, self-dis-cip-lin, and ree-uh-lis-tik exs-pek-tay-shunz.”
Uzbek meaning:
Bu mumkin, lekin bu atroflicha tanlov va aniq chegaralar talab etadi. Ko‘pchilik odamlar doimo mavjud bo‘lishga majbur bo‘ladilar, bu esa professional va shaxsiy mas’uliyatni bir-biriga aralashtirishi mumkin. Sog‘lom muvozanat vaqtni boshqarish, o‘zini tartibga solish va realistik umidlarga bog‘liq.

10) C1 — What are the consequences of relying too much on AI?
Question: What are the consequences of relying too much on AI?
Model answer:
One of the major consequences is reduced critical thinking. If people depend on AI for every decision, they may become less capable of analysing complex issues independently. In addition, there are concerns about bias, privacy, and accountability.
Pronunciation:
“wun of di may-jor kon-se-kwen-siz is ri-dyust kri-ti-kal thingk-ing. if pee-pul di-pen on ay-ay for ev-ree de-ci-zhun, they may bi-kum less kay-puh-bul of an-uh-lizing kom-pleks ish-uez in-de-pen-dent-lee. in ad-di-shun, der ar kon-sernz about bi-as, pri-vuh-see, and a-kount-uh-bil-i-tee.”
Uzbek meaning:
Asosiy oqibatlardan biri tanqidiy fikrlashning pasayishi. Agar odamlar har bir qaror uchun AI-ga bog‘lanib qolsa, ular murakkab muammolarni mustaqil tahlil qilish qobiliyatini yo‘qotishi mumkin. Bundan tashqari, tarafkashlik, maxfiylik va javobgarlik haqidagi xavotirlar ham mavjud.

11) C1 — Is it important to have a clear identity in a globalised world?
Question: Is it important to have a clear identity in a globalised world?
Model answer:
Yes, it is crucial. In a globalised world, people are exposed to different cultures and values, so a strong sense of identity helps them maintain direction and authenticity. At the same time, a healthy identity should be open and respectful of diversity.
Pronunciation:
“yes, it is kroo-shal. in a glo-buh-lized world, pee-pul ar ex-pozd tu dif-er-ent kul-churs and val-yuz, so a strong sens of ai-den-ti-tee helps dem main-tain di-rek-shun and au-ten-ti-ci-tee. at di saym time, a helthy ai-den-ti-tee shud bi o-pun and ri-spek-tful of di-ver-si-tee.”
Uzbek meaning:
Ha, bu juda muhim. Global dunyoda odamlar turli madaniyat va qadriyatlar bilan tanishadilar, shuning uchun aniq shaxsiy identitet ularga yo‘nalishni va haqiqiylikni saqlashga yordam beradi. Shu bilan birga, sog‘lom identitet ham ochiq va xilma-xillikka hurmatli bo‘lishi kerak.

12) C1 — How can education prepare students for uncertainty?
Question: How can education prepare students for uncertainty?
Model answer:
Education should not only transmit knowledge but also cultivate adaptability, resilience, and critical thinking. Students need to learn how to analyse information, solve unfamiliar problems, and work collaboratively in changing circumstances.
Pronunciation:
“ed-yu-kay-shun shud not on-lee trans-mit no-lij but ol-so kul-ti-vayt uh-dap-tuh-bil-i-tee, ri-zil-yens, and kri-ti-kal thingk-ing. stu-dents need tu lern how tu an-uh-lize in-for-may-shun, solv un-fam-yuh-lee prob-lems, and werk ko-lab-o-rat-iv-lee in chaynj-ing ser-kum-stan-siz.”
Uzbek meaning:
Ta’lim nafaqat bilim berishi, balki moslashuvchanlik, chidamlik va tanqidiy fikrlashni rivojlantirishi kerak. Talabalar ma’lumotni tahlil qilish, noma’lum muammolarni hal qilish va o‘zgaruvchan sharoitlarda hamkorlikda ishlashni o‘rganishi kerak.

13) C2 — To what extent should nations prioritise national interests over global cooperation?
Question: To what extent should nations prioritise national interests over global cooperation?
Model answer:
Nations should pursue national interests, but not at the expense of global stability. In an interconnected world, unilateral decisions can undermine trust, trade, and security. The most effective policies are those that balance national priorities with international responsibility.
Pronunciation:
“nay-shunz shud per-syu national in-ter-ests, bet not at di ex-pen-s of glo-bul sta-bil-i-tee. in an in-ter-kon-nekt-ed world, yoo-ni-lat-er-al de-si-zhunz ken un-der-mine trust, treyd, and se-kur-i-tee. di most ef-ek-tiv pol-i-siz ar doz dat bal-ans nay-shun-al pri-or-i-tiz wid in-ter-nash-un-al ri-spon-suh-bil-i-tee.”
Uzbek meaning:
Mamlakatlar milliy manfaatlarni ko‘zda tutishi kerak, lekin global barqarorlikka zarar yetkazadigan darajada emas. Bir-biriga bog‘langan dunyoda bir tomonlama qarorlar ishonch, savdo va xavfsizlikka zarar etkazishi mumkin. Eng samarali siyosat milliy ustuvorliklar bilan xalqaro mas’uliyatni muvozanatlashga qodir.

14) C2 — Is it possible to be completely objective in a world shaped by emotion and ideology?
Question: Is it possible to be completely objective in a world shaped by emotion and ideology?
Model answer:
Complete objectivity is difficult, if not impossible, because our perspectives are shaped by culture, experience, and values. However, intellectual honesty and methodological rigor can reduce bias and improve the quality of judgement.
Pronunciation:
“kom-pleet ob-jek-tiv-i-tee is dif-fi-kult, if not im-pos-si-bul, be-kaz our per-spek-tivz ar shaypd by kul-chur, ik-speer-ee-ens, and val-yuz. ha-ev-er, in-tel-ek-tu-al on-es-tee and meth-uh-dol-o-jik rig-or ken ri-dyus bi-as and im-proov di kwal-i-tee of judj-ment.”
Uzbek meaning:
Mutlaqo xolis bo‘lish qiyin, hatto imkonsiz, chunki bizning nuqtai nazarimiz madaniyat, tajriba va qadriyatlar bilan shakllanadi. Biroq, intellektual vijdonlilik va metodologik qat’iylik tarafkashlikni kamaytirib, hukmning sifatini yaxshilashi mumkin.

15) C2 — How should societies respond to mass migration?
Question: How should societies respond to mass migration?
Model answer:
Societies should respond with both compassion and pragmatic policy. Refugees and migrants need protection, legal recognition, and access to basic services, but governments also need to manage resources, public services, and social cohesion carefully. Long-term solutions require cooperation at local, national, and international levels.
Pronunciation:
“so-sai-i-tiz shud ri-spond wid both kom-pash-un and prag-mat-ik pol-i-see. ref-yu-jeez and mi-grants need pro-tek-shun, lee-gal rek-og-ni-shun, and ak-ses tu bay-sik ser-viz-es, bet gov-ern-ments ol-so need tu man-ij ri-sor-siz, pub-lik ser-viz-es, and so-shal ko-hee-zhun kar-ful-lee. long-term sol-yushunz ri-kwire ko-op-er-ay-shun at lo-kal, na-shun-al, and in-ter-nash-un-al lev-els.”
Uzbek meaning:
Jamiyatlar ham mehr-shafqat, ham amaliy siyosat bilan javob berishi kerak. Qochqinlar va migrantlar himoya, qonuniy tan olinganlik va asosiy xizmatlarga kirishni talab qiladi, ammo hukumatlar resurslar, davlat xizmatlari va ijtimoiy uyg‘unlikni ehtiyotkor boshqarishi kerak. Uzoq muddatli yechimlar mahalliy, milliy va xalqaro darajada hamkorlikni talab etadi.

16) C2 — Is cultural preservation compatible with modernisation?
Question: Is cultural preservation compatible with modernisation?
Model answer:
It can be, provided that societies treat culture not as a static relic but as a living system that evolves. Modernisation should not erase identity; rather, it should allow communities to reinterpret tradition in a way that remains meaningful in contemporary life.
Pronunciation:
“it ken bi, pro-vai-ded dat so-sai-i-tiz treyt kul-chur not az a stat-ik rel-ik but az a liv-ing sis-tem dat ev-olvz. mod-ern-i-zay-shun shud not i-rayz ai-den-ti-tee; ra-der, it shud al-low kom-mu-ni-tiz tu re-in-ter-pret tra-di-shun in a way dat ri-maynz mean-ing-ful in kon-tem-po-rer-ee laif.”
Uzbek meaning:
Bu mos bo‘lishi mumkin, agar jamiyatlar madaniyatni statik yodgorlik emas, balki rivojlanayotgan tirik tizim sifatida qarashsa. Modernizatsiya shaxsiyatni yo‘qotmasligi kerak; aksincha, jamiyatlarga an’analarni bugungi hayotda ma’noli tarzda qayta talqin qilish imkonini berishi kerak.

17) C2 — What is the role of language in cultural identity?
Question: What is the role of language in cultural identity?
Model answer:
Language is central to cultural identity because it carries memory, values, humour, and social norms. When a language declines, communities often lose not only a means of communication but also a framework for understanding history, belonging, and meaning.
Pronunciation:
“lang-gwij is sen-tral tu kul-chur-al ai-den-ti-tee be-kaz it kar-riz mem-o-ree, val-yuz, hum-or, and so-shal norms. when a lang-gwij de-kliynz, kom-mu-ni-tiz of-ten luse not on-lee a meenz of kom-yu-ni-kay-shun but ol-so a frame-work for un-der-stand-ing his-to-ree, be-long-ing, and mean-ing.”
Uzbek meaning:
Til madaniy identitetning markaziy qismidir, chunki u xotira, qadriyatlar, hazil va ijtimoiy me’yorlarni o‘z ichiga oladi. Agar til pasayib ketgan bo‘lsa, jamoalar nafaqat aloqa vositasini, balki tarix, o‘ziga mansublik va ma’no tushunchasini ham yo‘qotishi mumkin.

18) C2 — Should art be judged mainly by its social message or by artistic value?
Question: Should art be judged mainly by its social message or by artistic value?
Model answer:
Art should be assessed on both levels. A work of art may carry a powerful social message, but it must also possess formal qualities such as originality, composition, and emotional resonance. In other words, meaning and craftsmanship are inseparable.
Pronunciation:
“art shud bi as-sesd on both lev-els. a werk of art may kar-ree a pow-er-ful so-shal mes-ij, but it must ol-so pos-ses for-mal kwal-i-tiz such az o-rig-i-nal-i-tee, kom-po-zi-shun, and mo-shun-al res-o-nens. in uder words, mean-ing and craft-man-ship ar in-sep-ar-uh-bul.”
Uzbek meaning:
San’atni ikkala jihatdan ham baholash kerak. San’at asari kuchli ijtimoiy xabarni o‘z ichiga olishi mumkin, lekin unda orijinallik, kompozitsiya va hissiy ta’sir kabi formal sifatlarga ham ega bo‘lishi kerak. Boshqacha qilib aytganda, ma’no va hunarmandchilik ajratib bo‘lmaydigan birdir.

19) C2 — Can democracy function effectively without an informed public?
Question: Can democracy function effectively without an informed public?
Model answer:
No. Democracy depends on citizens being informed, engaged, and capable of evaluating competing claims. If the public is misinformed or disengaged, political institutions become vulnerable to manipulation, populism, and erosion of trust.
Pronunciation:
“no. de-mok-ra-see di-penz on sit-uh-zens bi-ing in-formd, en-gayjd, and kay-puh-bul of ev-al-yu-ay-ting kom-pet-ing kleymz. if di pub-lik is mis-in-formd or dis-en-gayjd, po-lit-i-kal in-sti-tu-shunz bi-kum vul-ner-uh-bul tu man-i-pu-lay-shun, pop-yu-lizm, and i-ro-zhun of trust.”
Uzbek meaning:
Yo‘q. Demokratiya fuqarolarning xabardor, faollik bilan ishtirok etuvchi va turli da’volarni baholash qobiliyatiga bog‘liq. Agar jamoatchilik xato ma’lumotga ega bo‘lsa yoki ishtirok etmasa, siyosiy institutlar manipulyatsiya, populizm va ishonchning zaiflashishiga moyil bo‘ladi.

20) C2 — What is the greatest challenge facing humanity in the next century?
Question: What is the greatest challenge facing humanity in the next century?
Model answer:
The greatest challenge is probably the ability to balance technological progress with human dignity and environmental sustainability. If we fail to do so, innovation may accelerate inequality, climate collapse, and social fragmentation. The task ahead is not merely scientific; it is moral and political.
Pronunciation:
“di greyt-est chal-enj is prob-uh-bly di uh-bil-i-tee tu bal-ans tech-nol-o-jik prog-ress wid hyu-man dig-ni-tee and en-vi-ron-men-tal sus-tay-nuh-bil-i-tee. if wi fail tu du so, in-no-vay-shun may ak-sel-er-ayt in-e-kwal-i-tee, kli-mat kol-aps, and so-shal frag-men-tay-shun. di task a-head is not mer-lee sci-en-tif-ik; it is mor-al and po-lit-i-kal.”
Uzbek meaning:
Eng katta muammo, ehtimol, texnik taraqqiyotni inson qadr-qimmati va ekologik barqarorlik bilan muvozanatlash qobiliyati bo‘ladi. Agar bu ishni uddalay olmasak, innovatsiyalar tengsizlik, iqlim inqirozi va ijtimoiy parchalanishni tezlashtirishi mumkin. Oldimizda turgan vazifa faqat ilmiy emas, balki axloqiy va siyosiy bo‘ladi.
